01 / ABOUT

A scientist focused on
questions that matter.

My work uses molecular and genomic approaches to understand the diversity, evolution and drug resistance of bacterial pathogens, with a particular focus on Mycobacterium tuberculosis.

I completed a PhD in Molecular Biology at Stellenbosch University in March 2025. My doctoral research examined how changes in tuberculosis treatment strategies related to the population structure of extensively drug-resistant tuberculosis in South Africa's Western Cape province.

I bring together laboratory experience, whole-genome sequencing and computational analysis to investigate pathogen populations and generate evidence that can inform infectious-disease research and public health.
